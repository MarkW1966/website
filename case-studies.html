Case Studies

Real examples of how I help people get unstuck, fix problems, and move forward.

1. Microsoft 365 Chaos → Clean and Stable

The problem: A small business had broken permissions, missing emails, and Teams channels nobody understood.

What I did: Cleaned the structure, fixed mail flow, rebuilt permissions, and removed the clutter.

The result: Everything worked again. No more sync issues. No more “where’s that file?”.

2. AI Overwhelm → Clear, Practical Use

The problem: A business owner was drowning in AI hype and didn’t know where to start.

What I did: Showed them how to use AI as a thinking tool, not a magic trick.

The result: They now use AI daily to plan, write, and make decisions — without confusion.

3. Messy Decisions → Visual Clarity

The problem: Someone had a big decision to make and couldn’t see the options clearly.

What I did: Built a simple visual map of the choices, risks, and outcomes.

The result: They made the decision confidently within the hour.
