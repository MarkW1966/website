Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to the things people usually ask before booking.

Do you work remotely?

Yes. Everything is remote. No travel, no delays, no hassle.

How long is a session?

Most sessions last around an hour. Some problems take less, some take a little longer.

What tools do you use?

Whatever you’re comfortable with — email, Teams, Zoom, or a simple phone call.

Do I need to prepare anything?

No. Just tell me what’s going wrong or what you’re stuck on. I take it from there.

Is this technical support?

Not in the traditional sense. I fix problems, yes — but the real value is clarity, structure, and getting things working properly.

How do I book?

Email me with a short description of what you need help with.
